HOW TO PLAY

  • Place towers along the path to pop incoming balloons.
  • Each balloon you let pass costs you a life.
  • Earn cash for each balloon popped. Use it to buy and upgrade towers.
  • Towers have two upgrade paths. You can only fully commit to one path per tower.
  • Some balloons have special properties (Camo, Lead, Regrow, Ceramic, Blimps).
  • Blimps (MOAB-class) are massive air enemies with huge HP - they appear in later rounds.
  • Different towers have different specialties - mix them up!
  • Survive 60 rounds to win in Standard mode, or push into Freeplay for endless action.
